# Asha customer workspace

The administrator starts with a customer, their balance, the next action, and recent calls. The page should be quiet enough to understand without instructions. Use the platform system font, neutral surfaces, comfortable spacing, and a single blue action color.

Preserve the established customer sidebar and account panel. Do not replace this with a table, a new headline, or a large upload area. Import Excel is a small action next to Call all; a file can also be dropped anywhere on the page. Import progress appears only when needed.

A call opens a focused detail sheet. Its outcome and next step come first. Transcript and recording remain expandable. Connection controls stay behind disclosure. Invoices and account activity are expandable. Payment entry remains clearly labelled and requires a reference. Search filters customers without changing the selected account.

Call all begins one pass through all eligible customer contacts immediately when the admin clicks, including future follow-ups. It can be repeated after each completed run. Call now is a compact button in the selected customer's next-action area and explicitly allows one early call before their scheduled follow-up. Disable it while a run or call is active. Keep Call all available even when no follow-ups are due. Never show the no-calls-due message as a reason to prevent a full rerun. The admin does not join audio. Importing or editing contacts never dials. Both actions respect saved stops and reconciled ledger balances. Preserve keyboard focus, native dialog dismissal, reduced motion and narrow layouts.
