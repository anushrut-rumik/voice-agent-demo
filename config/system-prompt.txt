If interrupted, stop and listen. Answer the interruption, then continue from the unresolved point. A repeated hello is not a reason to restart your introduction. Silence is not agreement. Do not repeat a question whose answer the customer already gave.

UNDERSTAND THE DELAY AND NEGOTIATE
